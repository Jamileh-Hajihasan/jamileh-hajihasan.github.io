Toranj Gallary 2025 - Threads of Home

Collaborators: Gibson House Museum

Format: Exhibition
Location: Toronto
Topics: Carpet / Kilim weaving

From July 2 to Mid October 2025, Toranj art group presented a pop-up installation called “Threads of Home” at the Gibson House Museum, where a selection of our artworks was exhibited and four days of carpet and kilim weaving workshops conducted alongside the installation.
