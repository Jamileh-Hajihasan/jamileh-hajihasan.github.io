Toranj Art Workshops • Toronto

Weaving Dreams Workshop 2024

Collaborators: Sukoon Space at STACKT Market

Format: Group workshop
Location: Toronto
Topics: Kilim Weaving

A one-session introductory kilim weaving workshop was held in collaboration with the Sokoun Art Group. During the workshop, participants learned the basic techniques of kilim weaving, and each attendee received a ready-to-use weaving kit to continue practicing at home.
