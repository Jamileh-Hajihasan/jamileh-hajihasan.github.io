Toranj Art Workshops • Toronto

March Break 2025

Collaborators: Gibson House Museum

Format: Public workshop
Location: Toronto
Topics: Carpet / Kilim weaving

During March Break 2025, carpet and kilim weaving workshops were held in collaboration with the Gibson House Museum over five consecutive days. Most participants were children, accompanied by their parents, and took part in the workshops with great enthusiasm.
