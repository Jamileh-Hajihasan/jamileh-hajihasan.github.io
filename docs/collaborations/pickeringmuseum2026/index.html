Pickering Museum - Kilim Weaving Workshop 2026

Collaborators: Pickering Museum

Format: Group Workshop
Location: Pickering Museum Village
Topics: Kilim weaving

In July and August 2026, we were delighted to partner with Pickering Museum Village to facilitate two well-attended group Kilim weaving workshops (July 12 & Aug 9). Hosted and ticketed by the museum through their website, these one-day sessions gave participants a hands-on introduction to basic Kilim weaving techniques.
