Group Workshop

Hands-on group workshops for community spaces, cultural events, and creative gatherings.

Good for

  • Beginner-friendly introductions
  • Short sessions (1–3 hours) or multi-week series
  • Team-building and cultural programming

In November 2024, an independent, single-session Tabby kilim weaving workshop was held at the Gibson House Museum. The program introduced participants to fundamental weaving techniques and included a complete weaving kit, offering a hands-on experience rooted in traditional textile practices.
