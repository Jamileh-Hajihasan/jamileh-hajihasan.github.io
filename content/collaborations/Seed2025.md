**Willowdale Nowruz Celebration 2025** \
**Collaborators:** Willowdale City Office and SEED Foundation

**Format:** Public workshop \
**Location:** Toronto \
**Topics:** Carpet / Kilim / Tapestry weaving

Toranj Team returned to the Nowruz Festival for the second time on March 1, 2025, partnering once again with Willowdale City Office and Seed Foundation. To enhance the experience, we introduced a pre-registration system, allowing participants to sign up early and choose their specific areas of interest.
