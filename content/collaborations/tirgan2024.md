      **Collaborators:** Willowdale City Office and Tirgan Organization 

      **Format:** Public workshop \
      **Location:** Toronto \
      **Topics:** Carpet / Kilim / Tapestry weaving 

      During the Nowruz Festival on March 9, 2024, our team had the pleasure of collaborating with Willowdale City Office and Tirgan Organization in holding an interactive Persian Carpet, Kilim and Tapestry workshop designed for attendees of all ages. Through individual training, we provided a brief overview of traditional weaving techniques, allowing participants to experience weaving the craft firsthand. The event fostered cultural exchange by introducing international visitors to Persian textiles while offering Iranian children a meaningful connection to their ancestral art and heritage.
