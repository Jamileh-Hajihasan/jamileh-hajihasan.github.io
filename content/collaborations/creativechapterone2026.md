      **Topics:** Persian Carpet and Kilim 

      On June 21st, we were honored to participate in a multi-disciplinary art exhibition hosted by Orchid Gallery at the Meridian Arts Centre. 

      Alongside a talented group of artists from various disciplines, we exhibited our latest collection of handwoven art. The event was a wonderful opportunity to showcase our craft, offer visitors a live hands-on look into the traditional technique of kilim weaving, and introduce our upcoming kilim weaving workshops at Orchid Gallery.
