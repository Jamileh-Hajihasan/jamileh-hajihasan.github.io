**Canada Day Celebration 2024** \
**Collaborators:** Gibson House Museum 

**Format:** Public workshop \
**Location:** Toronto \
**Topics:** Carpet / Kilim / Tapestry weaving

On Canada Day,2024, our team had the honor of collaborating with Gibson House Museum and holding an art workshop centered on traditional Iranian textiles. During this event, the arts of Persian carpet, kilim, and tapestry were introduced to the audience, and families were able to share a hands-on, inspiring experience of Iranian art together.
